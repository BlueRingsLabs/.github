# Welcome to Blue Rings Labs

Hi! I'm [@glromero](https://github.com/glromero), Founder of **Blue Rings Labs**. It's an independent Engineering, Research & Educational Lab.

**Blue Rings Labs** is the culmination of 15 years of preparation and experience in systems engineering, software, and AI. It offers everything from free resources—such as open-source projects and intensive courses in mathematics, ML/DL, and AI—to personalized consulting, penetration testing, and custom software development.
